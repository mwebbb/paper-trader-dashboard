Every buy gets a news verdict before entry. Trades are not changed by it; the figures show how flagged buys did against cleared ones.

Exit rules

A holding is sold in full at the session close when its score shows 2 or more exhaustion flags, 3 or more bearish flags, or 2 bearish flags inside a death cross. No stop losses.

What is live

The quote tape, the main chart and each row's intraday chart stream from TradingView in your browser. Equity, P&L and the spread are marked to ; the page checks for new marks every 5 minutes.
